This Villum Synergy Grant (approx. **4.5 million DKK** / **600,000 EUR**) funds the DK-Future research programme from 2026 to 2029. The grant includes **five Post-Doc years** and supports work at the intersection of probabilistic machine learning, spatio-temporal modelling, and GeoAI for Danish land-use forecasting under climate change. The project is carried out in close collaboration with **Jamal Jokar Arsanjani** from the **Planning Department at Aalborg University**.
